Our Story

About Baker'z Bite

We're a small Karachi bakery with a simple belief: the best treats are the ones baked fresh, shared generously, and made from ingredients you can actually pronounce. Every cupcake, cookie, and mini box that leaves our kitchen is made the same way it was on day one — by hand, with care.

The Heart of Every Bake

Baked With Purpose, Shared With Love

Baker'z Bite started in a home kitchen in 2015 with one oven, one recipe box, and a habit of giving away more cupcakes than we sold. What began as weekend baking for family and neighbors slowly turned into something people started asking for by name.

Today we're a full bakery — but the habit never left. We still use pure butter, organic flour, and single-origin chocolate, and everything is still baked in small batches every single morning so it reaches you at its freshest.
